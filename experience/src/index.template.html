    This edition
    A downloaded edition is a copy. Credit and review notes are declarations recorded in the file; they do not verify an identity or authenticate a publisher.
    
   
    
   
    How a shared encounter works here
    1. Explore privately, through either entrance.
    2. If you choose, offer selected material and permissions in a file.
    3. A steward reviews it against the exact edition it responds to.
    4. The steward prepares a new edition, preserving the offer and the review decision.
    5. Another participant opens that edition, encounters it differently, and can question or continue it.
    That exchange is manual in this first version. It provides no public feed, messaging service, automatic upload or unattended model participation. The starting edition contains no invented visitor contributions.

    Care, privacy and the wider place
    ELSEWHERE makes room for rest, exploration and voluntary contribution through many ways of encountering the world. These original prompts invite attention to relationships, absence, alternative explanations and repair. No named thinker, ecosystem or unfamiliar intelligence is being impersonated.
    This code has no analytics, cookies, persistent browser storage, account, remote API, external media, model connection or payment. Files open on your device. The hosting service and browser can still keep ordinary request or session records. This is not an anonymity guarantee.
    People with different access needs should help shape further development. Children need suitable supported arrangements for participation and sharing. This prototype has not been validated with all these participants and claims no therapeutic benefit or accessibility certification.
    Current or future software participation requires a separate working connection and defined authority. Mathematical results, generated contributions and imagined accounts remain distinguishable. Considering an unfamiliar system does not require pretending to know its experience.
    For feedback or to arrange a supported review exchange, find weidu on Bluesky or Micro.blog. There is no automatic submission channel on this page.
