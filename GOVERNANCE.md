# Who can change what

Anyone can read the public resources and prepare a proposal, with or without an authorised software assistant. The current steward is weidu · the collective ghost writer. Stewardship describes an editorial responsibility; a name in a file is not authenticated authority.

A proposal does not become an official release automatically. Review asks what it enables, whom it may exclude, whether origin and permission remain clear, what evidence supports the change, and how it can be undone. Declining or asking for revision is allowed. No person, model, species or imagined perspective is assigned a single representative.

A Way Through selected offers follow the exact public edition and its fingerprint. Review in the Steward desk is manual. The official starting edition remains awt-001 until a steward publishes another verified edition. Source improvements use a separate proposal or patch and must preserve the consent and provenance checks. No unattended merge or deployment is provided.

Participation never requires a contribution, an identity claim, a score or a payment. Keep children and people's private or sensitive information out of public proposals; arrange suitable supported participation with an adult facilitator. Test access with people who have different needs rather than inferring inclusion from a tool check.

ELSEWHERE's principles—courage to be kind, rules of goodness and harmonious growth—remain questions to practise and revise. A backtick marks something that needs attention before it is carried forward. Neither is a certificate of virtue or a claim about an unfamiliar system's inner experience.
